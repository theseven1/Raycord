## 1.0.0 (2026-11-24)

First public release.

### Features

- Profile section and full report with an overview, one card per source and linked Roblox accounts
- Sources come from Rayward's source list
- Each source is looked up, cached and retried on its own
- Source cards show status, reasons, evidence, review details and the appeal link
- Rotector's linked Roblox accounts, possible alts, tracked Discord servers and other Discord accounts on the same Roblox account
- Member list and chat markers, a warning under a flagged user's first message, and DM notifications
- `/rayward`, `/rayward-roblox` and `/rayward-group` slash commands
- Results are cached
